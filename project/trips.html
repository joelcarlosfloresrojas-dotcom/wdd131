Macchu Picchu - Tourist Logistics
Information Details for Travelers
How to Get There Train from Ollantaytambo to Aguas Calientes, then a 30-minute bus ride up the mountain.
Booking Requirements Tickets sell out fast. Must be booked months in advance. Original passport is required for entry.
Guide Policy An official tour guide is highly recommended (and sometimes strictly required) for your first entry.
Facilities & Rules No bathrooms inside the citadel (use them before entering). No tripods, drones, or walking sticks allowed.
Time Needed A standard guided circuit takes about 2.5 to 3 hours.
Inka Paths - Trekking Essentials
Information Details for Travelers
Permit Availability Strictly limited to 500 people per day (including porters). Must book 6+ months in advance.
Physical Demand High. Involves steep, uneven stone steps and high altitude. Pre-trip fitness training is highly advised.
Accommodation & Meals Tent camping for 3 nights. Meals are prepared by our trekking chefs.
Luggage Porters carry the main gear. You only need to carry a daypack with water, snacks, and a camera.
Closures The trail is entirely closed every February for heavy rain and maintenance.
Sacsayhuaman - Visitor Guide
Information Details for Travelers
Distance from City Center A 10-minute taxi ride, or a steep but scenic 30-minute walk from the Cusco Plaza de Armas.
Entry Ticket Requires the "Boleto Turístico" (Cusco Tourist Ticket). You cannot buy a single-entry ticket just for this site.
Best Time to Visit Early morning to beat the crowds, or late afternoon for a spectacular sunset view over the city of Cusco.
What to Bring Sunscreen, a hat, and a light jacket (the wind picks up in the afternoon).
Time Needed 1.5 to 2 hours to walk among the massive zig-zag walls and explore the viewpoints.
Seven Colours Mountain - What to Expect
Information Details for Travelers
Departure Time Tours start very early. Expect a hotel pickup between 3:30 AM and 4:30 AM.
Acclimatization Crucial. You must spend at least 2 full days in Cusco before attempting this hike to avoid severe altitude sickness.
Weather Conditions Extremely unpredictable. It can be sunny, snowing, and freezing all in one hour. Dress in warm layers.
Horse Rentals If you get tired, locals rent horses along the trail for an extra fee (covers about 80% of the uphill climb).
Safety & Support Our guides carry emergency oxygen tanks and first-aid kits at all times.
Ollantaytambo - Travel Tips
Information Details for Travelers
Strategic Location Home to the main train station. Many travelers explore these ruins right before catching the train to Machu Picchu.
Altitude Advantage At 2,792m, it is significantly lower than Cusco. It is a great place to stay your first few nights to breathe easier.
Entry Ticket Requires the "Boleto Turístico" (Cusco Tourist Ticket).
Physical Effort Exploring the main sun temple requires climbing over 200 steep, massive stone steps. Go slowly!
Local Vibe The town itself is a living Inca city with original water channels, cobblestone streets, and a great craft market.
