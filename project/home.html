Cuzco

Cuzco, nestled high in the Peruvian Andes, was once the majestic capital of the Inca Empire. Today, this UNESCO World Heritage site is a vibrant blend of ancient cobblestone streets and Spanish colonial architecture, serving as the perfect gateway to explore the Sacred Valley and the legendary ruins of Machu Picchu.
